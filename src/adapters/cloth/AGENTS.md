# Cloth adapter contract

- Preparation owns the pinned Three.js r132 Verlet simulation, one 24-second warm-up, eight continuous 24-second playback banks, source topology, camera transform, texture sampling, lighting and fog rows, fixture geometry, directional shadow projection, the single ground-plane transform, and playback frames.
- Runtime may only select the desktop or mobile prepared profile once from the 600px media query, select one cryptographically random starting bank, mount the prepared Morph package, retain the active bank plus one prefetched successor, publish prepared shape, shadow-leaf, and atlas-row state, and update the single responsive perspective carrier.
- Preserve the source defaults: 10 by 10 cloth cells, 25-unit rest distance, 0.03 damping, 0.1 mass, 981 times 1.4 gravity, 18 ms squared Verlet integration, structural constraints, wind enabled, the full top row pinned, and the ball hidden.
- The desktop retained graph uses 200 `<u>` corner-shape cloth triangles and 51 moving cloth-shadow leaves. The mobile profile bilinearly remeshes the same prepared source simulation to a full 6 by 6 square with 72 cloth triangles and 25 moving shadow leaves. Both profiles use source Lambert shading and Three.js r132 linear fog baked into exact-deduplicated opaque RGB prepared atlas rows, 30 static shadow leaves for the two poles, top bar, and two feet, one quality-90 WebP direct-image ground leaf whose pinned grass texels are inverse-mapped from source world UVs into the PolyCSS projective homography with the source texture's default `flipY`, a prepared anisotropic mip filter capped at 16 samples, a fitted depth-varying phase correction, source Lambert shading, and Three.js r132 linear fog baked into the pixels, and solid quad leaves for the fixtures. No runtime background tiling, Canvas, WebGL, WebGPU, SVG scene geometry, masks, clip paths, runtime polygon construction, runtime atlas rasterization, or DOM growth.
- Keep source checkouts, captures, and generated prepared assets ignored.
- Do not claim pixel parity without a fresh pinned-source frame sequence and browser diff.
