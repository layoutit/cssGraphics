# Galaxy source notice

The Galaxy adapter derives its preparation-time initialization,
integration, projection, source palette selection, and restart behavior from XScreenSaver's
`hacks/galaxy.c`, revision `906693799e4fb7581436590cf84ecb2d3c9186ba`, with
SHA-256 `801b7a7ff3749b032974b8dfe9021c2e3998645f138f9beec7e09037e36d66d9`.
The source is recorded as HPND in `notes/references/source-lock.json`.

Three-galaxy encounters are qualified from complete native generation-zero
states. Every retained candidate must bring all three galaxy pairs through a
material close passage and projected disc overlap; the ranking then favors
deeper all-pair collision, visible discs, and tidal dispersal. Product qualification
measures only the source interval that is actually presented, rejects incoming centers
closer than 96 prepared pixels to a camera edge or pairs closer than 140 pixels, requires
all three source-space centers to enter one shared 40-pixel span in the same native frame,
and requires sustained all-pair mixing plus coherent foreign-cohort orbiting as a
secondary-vortex signal. These are deterministic seed-selection rules; they do not
translate source trajectories or add browser physics.

The three presentation roles are derived from the qualified lead seed's native
source hues: magenta and cyan preserve those source hue/chroma identities, and
the remaining source hue contributes only a slight tint to a neutral off-white
role. Preparation balances the two chromatic centers in OKLab lightness, gives
the off-white role an explicit luminance lead for small-point readability, adds
deterministic within-family hue variance, and rejects any RGB8
variant below a 7:1 black-background contrast floor. These are presentation
colors; the generated catalog retains every native RGB value, role assignment,
presentation center, and five-color family.

The source performs an abrupt randomized `startover()` after 1,001 frames. At
the user's direction, this prototype presents a curated endless reset. Each
encounter plays native generation-zero frames 0–409 over nine seconds,
then uses a prepared three-second velocity-matched cubic bridge to reform the same
retained leaves into the next three discs. Nothing fades, freezes, or rewinds.
The 10 highest-scoring qualified twelve-second encounters span five full 24-second transport banks. Startup
uses a session-persistent shuffled bag so every prepared encounter is chosen
once before a starting seed repeats. This is a
deliberate presentation override, not a claim of native restart parity. The runtime camera is fixed;
the source projection is already baked into point transforms. Runtime does not
simulate, recolor, calculate opacity, move the camera, or rebuild DOM. Palette
and constant opacity are static snapshot CSS rather than frame payload.
